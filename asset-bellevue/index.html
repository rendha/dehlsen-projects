Asset Bellevue, Pallimukku, Kochi · rooftop solar layout

R Madhavan Nair Rd, Pallimukku, Kochi (9.9638° N, 76.2862° E). Roof outline measured from Google Maps satellite imagery. Modules: 620 Wp TOPCon, 2278 × 1134 mm.

What's on the roof (traced + site video)

Roof about 32.3 m long and 14.0 m deep at the east end, long side about 14° off east–west, with a diagonal north-east edge. Two structures at the west end:

Lower box (overhead water tank), about 10.2 × 6.8 m, +3.0 m: flat concrete roof with an access hatch, water pipes and cable conduits running across.
Lift machine room, about 5.0 × 6.2 m, +4.5 m: door and canopy facing the pergola; dish, antenna mast and red aviation warning light on the roof; railing along the top.

Solar pergola · 10 modules · +6.2 kWpPanels on top of the existing pergola beside the lift room (5 × 2, 10°). Total 37 × 620 Wp = 22.9 kWp, comfortably over 20 kW. It gets some shade from the lift room in the late afternoon. Toggle it with "+ Solar pergola".

What's needed up there

Ladder access to both boxes, guard rails on the box edges (orange), frames bolted with sealed anchors, and panels rated for high uplift. The existing small panel on the tank box and the hose would be moved, and the tank hatch, lift room door, dish, mast and aviation light must stay clear and visible.

Kept free

The open terrace, pergola, water tanks and shaft all stay clear.
